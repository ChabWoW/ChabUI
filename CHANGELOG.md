## [0.1.0] - 2026-01-28

### Added
- ClearHiddenQuests: Automatically unwatches hidden quests
- BCDMAnchors: Adds ElvUI frame anchors to Better Cooldown Manager
- CurrentExpansionOnlyAuctionHouseFilter: Defaults auction house to current expansion
- SlashWA: Adds `/cd` and `/wa` commands to toggle cooldown viewer
- EndeavorProgressText: Shows progress text on mouseover for neighborhood initiatives
- SharedMedia_Chab: Custom media assets (borders, fonts, statusbars)
